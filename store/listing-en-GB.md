## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Count Your Dhikr. Connect Your Heart.

Misbah is a simple, focused tasbih counter for your daily dhikr and remembrance. There are no ads, no accounts and nothing to distract you: just a single button to count, and a quiet screen that keeps your place.

TAP TO COUNT
Tap the large button to add one. Each tap gives a short vibration, so you can keep counting without looking at the screen.

SET A GOAL
Set a goal such as 33, 99 or 100, and a gold ring fills as you count towards it, showing how many are left. When you reach your goal, counting pauses and a short celebration marks the moment. Then set a new goal or carry on without one. Either way, the count starts again from zero.

COUNT FREELY
No goal needed. Leave the goal empty and count for as long as you like.

GENTLE VIBRATION
A light buzz on every count and a stronger double pulse when you reach your goal. You can turn vibration off at any time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Misbah works fully offline. It does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your goal and vibration setting stay on your phone.

THOUGHTFUL DETAILS
• Large, easy-to-reach count button
• Calm deep-green and gold design
• Works with screen readers and larger text sizes
• Respects your phone's reduce-motion setting
• Reset in one tap

Misbah, meaning lamp, is made to help you keep your remembrance steady, one count at a time.
