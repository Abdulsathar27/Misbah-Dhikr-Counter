Privacy Policy

Misbah: Tasbih & Dhikr · Effective 11 November 2026

Misbah is a tasbih counter for dhikr. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: Misbah does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Misbah has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not ask for, read or send your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences, Misbah saves two settings on your phone only:

  • the goal you set, if any, and
  • whether vibration is switched on.

These never leave your device. Your count is not saved and starts at zero each time you open the app. Uninstalling Misbah removes these settings. Your phone's own backup feature may include them in your personal device backup, which is controlled by you and your device provider.

Internet and permissions

Misbah works fully offline and does not connect to the internet. The only permission it uses is vibration, to give a short buzz when you count. This permission does not give access to any of your data.

Sharing

Because Misbah does not collect personal data, it has nothing to share or sell, and no third-party services receive data from the app.

Children's privacy

Misbah is suitable for all ages, including children. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and contains no ads or links that take children out of the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
